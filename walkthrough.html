STORY MODE WALKTHROUGH

Complete guide to every chapter with boss strategies

📖 CHAPTER 1: THE Beginning

Prologue: One For All Awakening

Tutorial chapter. Learn basic controls and combos.

  • • Battle 1: Training Battle vs Bakugo (Tutorial)
  • • Learn: Basic attacks, jumping, dash
  • • Unlock: Story Mode Chapter 1

📖 CHAPTER 1: U.A. SPORTS FESTIVAL

Battle 1: Tournament Bracket

Fight through preliminary rounds to reach the finals.

  • • Match 1: vs Mineta (Easy) - Ground game focus
  • • Match 2: vs Iida (Medium) - Speed vs Speed
  • • Match 3: vs Kirishima (Medium) - Defensive battle

Battle 2: Finals - vs Todoroki

The main event. Face Shoto Todoroki in the finals.

  • • Phase 1: Ice attacks only - Stay mobile
  • • Phase 2: Fire + Ice - Watch for temperature shifts
  • • Strategy: Use aerial combos to avoid ice floors

📖 CHAPTER 2: VILLAIN ACADEMY

Battle 1: vs Nomu

Encounter the terrifying Nomu creatures.

  • • Multiple Nomu appear - Focus one at a time
  • • Use assist characters for combo extensions
  • • Watch for shockwave attacks

Boss Battle: vs Shigaraki

Your first major boss battle.

  • • Phase 1: Standard Shigaraki - Learn patterns
  • • Phase 2: Decay area denial - Keep moving
  • • Phase 3: Rage mode - Aggressive offense needed

📖 CHAPTER 3: STAIN ARC

Battle 1: vs Tetsutetsu

Iron-like warrior. Match his steel with your speed.

Boss Battle: vs Stain

The Hero Killer challenges you.

  • • Parry his blade attacks - Counter with combos
  • • Avoid blood transfusion - Stay at range when possible
  • • His focus gauge depletes slowly - Be patient

💡 BOSS BATTLE TIPS

Pattern Recognition

Every boss has attack patterns. Observe and learn before attacking.

Save Assist Charges

Boss phases often require burst damage. Save assists for those moments.

Character Switching

Switch characters during cutscenes to build ultimate gauge faster.
