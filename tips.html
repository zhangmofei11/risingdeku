💥 COMBO EXTENSIONS

Air Chain Combos

Link aerial attacks into ground combos for extended damage.

  • • Jump cancel after any ground hit
  • • Air attack → fast fall → ground combo
  • • Maximum 6-hit air chain

Counter Hit Chains

Hits during opponent's attack have bonus stun.

  • • Attack during opponent's startup frames
  • • Counter hits add 3 extra frames of stun
  • • Chain into full combo from counter hit

🔄 CHARACTER SWITCHING

When to Switch

  • • After knocking opponent down - safe switch window
  • • During ultimate animation - invincibility frames
  • • When countered - immediate switch to punish
  • • Build ultimate gauge with assists

Switch Cancel Tech

  • • Cancel recovery frames with switch
  • • Creates mixup on wakeup
  • • Resets pressure sequence
  • • Costs 25% ultimate gauge

⚡ ULTIMATE ABILITY TIPS

Gauge Building

Land attacks, use assists, take damage - all build ultimate gauge. Maximize with character switching.

Safe Activation

Activate during knockback, after throw, or during switch cancel. Avoid predictable patterns.

Combo Integration

End combos with ultimate for maximum damage. Most ultimates deal 40%+ damage in optimized combos.

🛡️ DEFENSIVE TECHNIQUES

Parry Timing

Parry window is 8 frames before attack lands. Practice against specific characters to learn timings.

Reflect Cancel

Reflect during pressure to create distance. Costs 1 bar but is plus on block.

Wakeup Options

Delay tech, instant air recovery, and wakeup attack are your main wakeup options. Mix to avoid predictable patterns.

Punishment

Know your character's best punish combo. Block dangerous moves and whiff punish with full combo.
